# Florida NextRyde

**Florida Ride Reservations Operating Under the Florida Reimbursement Model**

Florida NextRyde is a ride reservation platform connecting Florida passengers with independent Florida drivers who voluntarily provide transportation and receive reimbursement for vehicle-related expenses rather than compensation as a for-hire transportation carrier.

## Overview

Florida NextRyde is designed around a reimbursement model where drivers use their personal vehicles and may receive reimbursement for transportation-related expenses. The platform is intended to facilitate ride reservations while avoiding operation as a traditional taxi, limousine, transportation network company (TNC), or common carrier.

Drivers remain responsible for determining their own compliance with applicable federal, state, and local laws.

## How the Reimbursement Model Works

1. A passenger submits a ride reservation.
2. A participating driver may accept the reservation.
3. The driver provides transportation using their personal vehicle.
4. The passenger may reimburse the driver for vehicle expenses such as:
   - Fuel
   - Mileage
   - Tolls
   - Parking
   - Vehicle wear and tear
5. The reimbursement is intended to offset transportation expenses rather than provide wages.

## Restrictions

### Drivers Must

- Possess a valid driver's license.
- Maintain required automobile insurance.
- Operate a legally registered vehicle.
- Comply with all applicable traffic laws.
- Comply with local ordinances and regulatory requirements.

### Drivers Must Not

- Represent themselves as employees of Florida NextRyde.
- Hold themselves out as a taxi company through the platform.
- Accept transportation requests prohibited by law.
- Transport passengers in violation of insurance restrictions.

### Platform Limitations

Florida NextRyde:

- Is a reservation platform.
- Does not own vehicles.
- Does not employ drivers unless specifically stated.
- Does not guarantee reimbursement amounts.
- Does not provide legal, tax, or regulatory advice.
- Does not guarantee that driver activity qualifies for any particular legal exemption.
